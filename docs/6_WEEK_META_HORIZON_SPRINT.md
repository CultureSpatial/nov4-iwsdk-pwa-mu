## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
**Risk:** Meta Horizon Engine migration complexity
- **Mitigation:** Start with simplest experience (photo booth)
- **Fallback:** Use Universal AR as proven mobile solution

**Risk:** Cloud streaming performance issues
- **Mitigation:** Optimize early, test on low-end devices
- **Fallback:** Progressive web app with deep linking

### Timeline Risks
**Risk:** 6 weeks is aggressive
- **Mitigation:** Focus on photo booth first, cocktail menu second
- **Fallback:** Launch photo booth in 4 weeks, cocktails in 8

### Approval Risks
**Risk:** Meta rejects contest submission
- **Mitigation:** Follow all guidelines, high production quality
- **Fallback:** Still get catalog listing and distribution
