**Project Name**: Meta Ray-Ban Stories × We Rise Wine Shop

## INNOVATIVE GEAR STORYTELLING

### The Meta Ray-Ban Stories Advantage (Not Limitation)

**Core Message**: "No AR overlay, no HUD—just authentic point-of-view capture that serves storytelling, not replaces it."

### Key Differentiators

#### 1. Hands-Free, Head-Mounted Capture
**What This Enables**:
- Natural hand movements (pouring, handling objects, gesturing)
- Authentic eye-level perspective
- Multi-tasking (holding wine glass while capturing)
- Seamless audio with onboard mic array

**Shots That Showcase This**:
- **S05**: Ray-Ban POV scan—host aims glasses naturally at bottle
- **S06**: Flight add-to-kit—hands free to interact with phone AND wine
- **S07**: Stripe checkout—glasses capture entire transaction moment

**Expanded Screens/Notes**:
- [ ] Comparison: Ray-Ban vs. phone gimbal (framing stability)
- [ ] Comparison: Ray-Ban vs. chest-mounted GoPro (perspective authenticity)
- [ ] Ray-Ban unique framing strategy doc (15° right of center guidance)
- [ ] Multi-angle capture strategy for critical moments

#### 2. No Display = No Distraction
**What This Enables**:
- Wearer must be present (not staring at screen)
- Authentic eye contact with people
- Natural reactions (not mediated by display)
- Technology disappears into moment

**Shots That Showcase This**:
- **Shot 105**: Sommelier introduction—natural eye contact during conversation
- **Shot 110**: Toast celebration—genuine connection, not screen-mediated
- **Shot 108**: Producer story—speaker's expression captured authentically

**Expanded Screens/Notes**:
- [ ] Wearer briefing: "Forget about the camera, be present"
- [ ] Staff briefing: "Glasses are invisible, just talk naturally"
- [ ] Post-shoot debrief: Capture testimonials about authenticity
- [ ] Comparison footage: Glasses vs. phone-based capture (side-by-side)

#### 3. Audio Quality (Five-Mic Array)
**What This Enables**:
- Spatial audio capture
- Clear dialogue in ambient environment
- ASMR-quality sound (pouring, glass clinks, ambient)
- Natural conversation recording (vs. lapel mic)

**Shots That Showcase This**:
- **Shot 102**: Greeting exchange—captured with full audio warmth
- **Shot 105**: Sommelier conversation—five-mic advantage visible in clean audio
- **Shot 106**: Wine pour—ASMR pouring sound
- **Shot 110**: Toast—glass clinks and phrase capture

**Expanded Screens/Notes**:
- [ ] Audio comparison: Raw glasses mic vs. lapel mic vs. ambient mix
- [ ] Five-mic positioning diagram
- [ ] Dialogue clarity test checklist
- [ ] ASMR sound library (pour, clink, ambient wine bar)

#### 4. LED Indicator Lights (Visible in Footage)
**What This Enables**:
- Transparency: Audience sees glasses are recording
- Trust: No hidden cameras
- Aesthetic: Modern tech element
- Compliance: Clear indicator of recording status

**Shots That Showcase This**:
- **S05**: Ray-Ban POV scan—red record LED visible in mirror reflection
- **Shot U02**: Unboxing close-up—LED positioning on glasses
- **B-roll**: LED light variations (standby, recording, low battery)

**Expanded Screens/Notes**:
- [ ] LED light meaning reference card
- [ ] Mirror reflection capture technique guide
- [ ] LED visibility test checklist
- [ ] Low-light LED capture adjustment notes

#### 5. Framing Strategy (No Display Workaround)
**Shots That Work Reliably**:
- ✓ Hands-in-frame activities (wine pouring, tasting, handling objects)
- ✓ Forward-facing walks (approaching venue, exploring)
- ✓ Social interactions (conversations, person positioned right-of-center)
- ✓ Environmental scanning (slow 180° pans)
- ✓ Audio-focused content (toasts, greetings, storytelling)

**Shots Requiring Backup Camera**:
- ⚠ Precise framing (unboxing exterior shots)
- ⚠ Wide establishing shots (spatial context)
- ⚠ Two-shots of conversations (POV shows only one perspective)

**Expanded Screens/Notes**:
- [ ] Framing calibration process (15° right of center guide)
- [ ] Wearer briefing: Head position and eye-line technique
- [ ] Camera operator backup responsibility checklist
- [ ] Post-shoot review process for framing accuracy

## SENSE OF ARRIVAL

### Scene 2 Intent
Create "welcome moment" that establishes venue warmth and invites participation. All Ways Friendly playbook element.

### Psychological Architecture
The "sense of arrival" is the critical handoff moment between unboxing (product focus) and wine discovery (experience focus). This is where glasses become invisible and hospitality becomes visible.

### Shot Breakdown

#### Shot 101: Exterior Approach POV
**Duration**: 20 seconds  
**Setup**: POV from glasses wearer approaching wine shop

**Visual**:
- Walking toward wine shop entrance (shot from eye level)
- Natural head movement showing neighborhood context
- Shop signage becoming visible as approach
- Storefront windows, outdoor seating (if available)

**Audio**:
- Ambient street sounds (footsteps, traffic, city ambience)
- Natural environmental audio (if music playing outside)
- No voiceover

**BC Tourism Tie-in**: "More in a Match" journey theme
- If possible, capture Vancouver skyline/mountains in background
- Show neighborhood context (this is part of broader BC wine exploration)
- Frame as "arrival at gateway venue"

**Key Message**: This is not a transaction—it's an invitation to explore.

**Framing Strategy**:
- Host positioned at eye level throughout walk
- Smooth, natural head turns (not jerky)
- Occasional dialogue partner might be off to right side (accommodating no-display framing)
- 15° right of center adjustment if walking with companion

**Expanded Notes**:
- [ ] Pre-shoot walk-through to identify best approach angle
- [ ] Lighting check (time of day for golden hour if possible)
- [ ] Sound ambience quality assessment (street noise, wind)
- [ ] Neighborhood context documentation (signage, surroundings)
- [ ] Safety walk-through (pedestrian route, traffic patterns)

---

#### Shot 102: Entrance & Greeting
**Duration**: 15 seconds  
**Setup**: Host entering wine shop, greeting staff

**Visual**:
- Hand opening door (captured in POV from glasses)
- First glimpse of interior
- Staff greeting moment (staff positioned right-of-center in frame)
- Natural facial expressions and welcome gesture
- Shop interior revealed progressively

**Audio**:
- Door opening sound (foley or natural)
- Natural greeting exchange
- Ambient interior sound (light music, background activity)

**Audio Recording Highlight**: This is the mic array's moment to shine—capturing warmth and authenticity.

If you're comfortable, you can look toward the glasses like you're saying hi to [Host]. The most important thing is that your greeting feels genuine."
